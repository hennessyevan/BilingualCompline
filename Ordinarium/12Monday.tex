\bihangingpsalm{
Custódi ánimam meam, quóniam \textbf{sanc}tus sum;\redstar\\
salvum fac servum tuum, Deus meus, spe\mbox{\textit{rántem} \textbf{in} te.}

Miserére mei, \textbf{Dó}mine,\redstar\\
quóniam ad te clamávi \textit{tota} \textbf{di}e.

Lætífica ánimam servi \textbf{tu}i,\redstar\\
quóniam ad te, Dómine, ánimam me\textit{am} \textit{le}\textbf{vá}vi.

Quóniam tu, Dómine, suávis et \textbf{mi}tis\redstar\\
et multæ misericórdiæ ómnibus invo\textit{cánti}\textbf{bus} te.

Auribus pércipe, Dómine, oratiónem \textbf{me}am\redstar\\
et inténde voci deprecati\textit{ónis} \textbf{me}æ.

In die tribulatiónis meæ clamávi \textbf{ad} te,\redstar\\
quia ex\textit{áudi}\textbf{es} me.

Non est símilis tui in diis, \textbf{Dó}mine,\redstar\\
et nihil sicut ó\textit{pera} \textbf{tu}a.

Omnes gentes, quascúmque fecísti, vénient\reddagger\\
et adorábunt coram te, \textbf{Dó}mine,\redstar\\
et glorificábunt \textit{nomen} \textbf{tu}um,

quóniam magnus es tu et fáciens mira\textbf{bí}lia:\redstar\\
tu es \textit{Deus} \textbf{so}lus.

Doce me, Dómine, viam \textbf{tu}am,\redstar\\
et ingrédiar in veri\textit{táte} \textbf{tu}a;

simplex fac cor \textbf{me}um,\redstar\\
ut tímeat \textit{nomen} \textbf{tu}um.

Confitébor tibi, Dómine Deus meus, in toto corde \textbf{me}o\redstar\\
et glorificábo nomen tuum \textit{in} \textit{æ}\textbf{tér}num,

quia misericórdia tua magna est \textbf{su}per me,\redstar\\
et eruísti ánimam meam ex inférno in\textit{feri}\textbf{ó}ri.

Deus, supérbi insurrexérunt super me,\reddagger\\
et synagóga poténtium quæsiérunt ánimam \textbf{me}am,\redstar\\
et non proposuérunt te in con\textit{spéctu} \textbf{su}o.

Et tu, Dómine, Deus miserátor et mi\textbf{sé}ricors,\redstar\\
pátiens et multæ misericórdiæ et \textit{veri}\textbf{tá}tis,

\vspace{\baselineskip}
réspice in me et miserére mei;\reddagger\\
da fortitúdinem tuam púero \textbf{tu}o\redstar\\
et salvum fac fílium an\textit{cíllæ} \textbf{tu}æ.

Fac mecum signum in bonum,\reddagger\\
ut vídeant, qui odérunt me, et confun\textbf{dán}tur,\redstar\\
quóniam tu, Dómine, adiuvísti me et conso\mbox{\textit{látus} \textbf{es} me.}

Glória Patri, et \textbf{Fí}lio,\redstar\\
et Spirí\textit{tui} \textbf{Sanc}to.

Sicut erat in princípio, et nunc, et \textbf{sem}per,\redstar\\
et in sǽcula sæcu\textit{lórum}. \textbf{A}men.
}
{
Preserve my life, for I am \textbf{faith}ful;\redstar\\
save the ser\textit{vant who} \textbf{trusts} in you.

You are my God, have mercy \textbf{on} me, Lord,\redstar\\
for I cry to you \textit{all the} \textbf{day} long.

Give joy to your servant, \textbf{O} Lord,\redstar\\
for to you I \textit{lift up} \textbf{my} soul.

O Lord, you are good and for\textbf{giv}ing,\redstar\\
full of \textit{love to} \textbf{all} who call.

Give heed, O Lord, \textbf{to} my prayer\redstar\\
and attend to the \textit{sound of} \textbf{my} voice.

In the day of distress \textbf{I} will call\redstar\\
and sure\textit{ly you} \textbf{will} reply.

Among the gods there is none like \textbf{you}, O Lord;\redstar\\
nor work \textit{to com}\textbf{pare} with yours.

All the nations shall come to a\textbf{dore} you\redstar\\
and glori\textit{fy your} \textbf{name}, O Lord:

\vspace{\baselineskip}
for you are great and do marvel\textbf{lous} deeds,\redstar\\
you \textit{who a}\textbf{lone} are God.

Show me, Lord, your way\reddagger\\
so that I may walk \textbf{in} your truth.\redstar\\
Guide my \textit{heart to} \textbf{fear} your name.

\vspace{\baselineskip}
I will praise you, Lord my God, with \textbf{all} my heart\redstar\\
and glorify your \textit{name for} \textbf{ev}er;

for your love to me \textbf{has} been great:\redstar\\
you have saved me from the \textit{depths of} \textbf{the} grave.

The proud have risen against me;\reddagger\\
ruthless men \textbf{seek} my life;\redstar\\
to \textit{you they} \textbf{pay} no heed.

But you, God of mercy and com\textbf{pas}sion,\redstar\\
slow to \textit{anger}, \textbf{O} Lord,

abounding in \textbf{love} and truth,\redstar\\
turn and take \textit{pity} \textbf{on} me.

O give your strength to your \textbf{ser}vant\redstar\\
and \textit{save your} \textbf{hand}maid’s son.

Show me a sign of your favour\reddagger\\
that my foes may see \textbf{to} their shame\redstar\\
that you console me and \textit{give me} \textbf{your} help.

Glory be to the Father and \textbf{to} the Son\redstar\\
and to the \textit{Holy} \textbf{Spi}rit,

as it was in the beginning, is now, and ever \textbf{shall} be,\redstar\\
world with\textit{out end}. \textbf{A}men.
}
